    About

    Zebra Films LLC is led by Temitope Olutunmbi, an editor who has spent over ten years in post-production. His work spans network series and promos for Comedy Central, VH1 and VICE, brand work for Nike and NationSwell, and independent narrative shorts made with directors he believes in.

    Whatever the budget, the job is the same: find the story in the footage and deliver it on time.

    Temitope also writes and makes work about film and AI at temiolutunmbi.com.
